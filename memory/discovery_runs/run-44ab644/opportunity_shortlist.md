# Opportunity Shortlist

## Research constraints

- Taiwan-first; buyers must be findable online without cold calling/email as the primary channel.
- Default test scope: 1–3 people, NT$0–5,000, up to 3 months, manual-first.
- This run does not recommend building software or running a paid pilot.
- Commercial stages are separated: `interest`, `buyer_request`, `commitment`, `paid_transaction`.
- A marketplace listing with a budget is a `buyer_request`, not proof of payment. A completed buyer review may be `paid_transaction` only when the text clearly describes completed service.
- Previous run preserved at `memory/discovery_runs/run-c5c2548/`; older runs remain under their own archive directories.

## Top Startup Opportunities

### Candidate A — 台灣微型品牌的「電商後台例行營運包」

- Startup classification: `startup-ready-to-test` (medium confidence; exact package still unproven).
- Target user: 只有 1–3 人、同時經營官網與蝦皮/momo/PChome 等通路的台灣小品牌。
- Economic buyer: 品牌負責人、營運主管或電商負責人。
- Problem: 商品上架/資料維護、訂單與出貨、客服、庫存/退貨與活動資料需要持續處理；小團隊以內部雇員或廣泛代營運服務承擔，容易變成每天的例行補位工作。
- Current workaround: 內部電商營運助理、兼職人員、綜合型代營運商、Excel/平台後台人工操作。
- Strongest evidence:
  - **Observed / paid_transaction / Tier A, 2026-09:** PRO360 電商代營運頁面列出近期客戶評價；其中一則明確表示雇用代營運後理解如何正確經營且業績持續成長，另有「目前是線上個人接案」等完成服務語境。頁面也說 TOP PRO 需半年內完成五筆以上交易及客戶評價，且平台提供合約與第三方支付流程。這證明類別存在已完成外包交易，不證明本輪窄化的固定工作包已被買。 [Source](https://www.pro360.com.tw/category/ec_third_partner)
  - **Observed / buyer_request / Tier A, 2026-04:** Tasker buyer requests long-term e-commerce operations including product listing, customer complaints, payment/logistics coordination and inventory; another request asks for a monthly fee plus performance share. [Source](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=133%2C140%2C447)
  - **Observed / paid_labor / Tier A, 2026-08/09:** 104 listings repeatedly assign product listing, platform maintenance, inventory, orders, returns, customer service and sales-data tracking; listed compensation includes NT$35,000–55,000/month and other roles. [Source](https://www.104.com.tw/jobs/search/?keyword=%E9%9B%BB%E5%95%86)
- External demand: **Observed** for broad e-commerce outsourcing; exact fixed package remains **Unknown**.
- Payment/commitment: **Paid transaction observed for comparable category** through completed customer-review evidence; payment for this exact package is **unproven**.
- Evidence quality: A/B mix; completed category transactions plus recent buyer requests and paid labor.
- Value mechanism: measurable response/processing time, fewer missed updates, fewer inventory/order errors, and reduced owner interruptions. Exact savings are Unknown.
- Why now: current listings show small brands needing operational coverage, while the category already has online transaction infrastructure and remote delivery.
- Repeatability: recurrence trigger = daily/weekly operations, campaign cycles and inventory/order exceptions. Expected recurrence type = `same-customer` and `cross-customer`.
- Standardizable unit: one or two platform channels, fixed SKU/order volume band, response SLA, listing/update checklist, exception queue and weekly summary.
- Leverage path: standard operating procedures, batch imports, templates, trained operators and selective platform automation. Founder-labor scaling risk = `medium-high` until scope excludes bespoke marketing and the exception rate is known.
- Main contradiction: broad “代營運” is already crowded and often includes strategy, creatives, ads and performance accountability; a narrow operations package may be too small or unattractive to buyers.
- Biggest unknown: whether a buyer will accept a fixed recurring scope instead of hiring a generalist or buying full-service management.
- Cheapest behavioral test: publish a fixed-scope recurring offer for one platform and one operational bundle through an existing marketplace; ask for an accepted quote or scheduled start date, without building software or promising growth outcomes. Success signal: 3 qualified inquiries, 1 concrete commitment, and the same task boundaries repeated across at least 2 buyers.
- Kill criterion: every buyer asks for ads/creative/strategy/custom customer service; no fixed scope is accepted; or founder hours grow linearly with order volume.

### Candidate B — 多通路電商的月結、發票與退款差異包

- Startup classification: `startup-ready-to-test` (medium confidence; payment for exact wedge unproven).
- Target user: 官網＋多通路銷售的台灣小品牌、代理商或電商團隊。
- Economic buyer: 品牌負責人、財務負責人或營運主管。
- Problem: 每月要彙整各平台、金流、銀行、ERP 與發票資料，處理退款/折讓和差異，再交給記帳士或會計師；現有外包記帳服務可能不涵蓋營運端例外整理。
- Current workaround: 內部電商會計、Excel/CSV 匯出人工核對、外包記帳士/會計師接手。
- Strongest evidence:
  - **Observed / buyer_request / Tier A, 2026-08-28:** Taiwan Tasker buyer explicitly requests long-term outsourcing after cancelling an internal finance role; scope includes multi-channel invoices, reconciliation, refunds/allowances, monthly reports and accountant handoff. The listing shows NT$6,000 and 15 proposals, which remain request-stage evidence only. [Source](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=250%2C60%2C64)
  - **Observed / paid_labor / Tier A, 2026-06/07:** 104 listings assign platform settlement, bank/platform reconciliation, invoices, refunds/allowances and monthly close; visible monthly salaries include NT$30,000–33,000 and NT$45,000–60,000. [Source](https://www.104.com.tw/jobs/search/?jobcat=2003001000&keyword=%E5%B0%8D%E5%B8%B3%E5%96%AE)
  - **Observed / paid_transaction / Tier A, current page with 2026 review:** PRO360 accounting outsourcing page displays completed customer reviews and describes recurring external bookkeeping relationships; this is comparable accounting outsourcing, not proof that the operational reconciliation wedge was purchased. [Source](https://www.pro360.com.tw/category/accounting_service)
- External demand: **Observed** for outsourced accounting and one explicit e-commerce reconciliation request; exact fixed package still **Unknown**.
- Payment/commitment: comparable bookkeeping **paid transaction observed**; exact multi-channel reconciliation payment **unproven**.
- Evidence quality: A/B mix; strong recent workflow/payroll evidence and conservative externalization.
- Value mechanism: hours avoided, fewer monthly exceptions, faster handoff and lower error/leakage risk; no savings number asserted.
- Repeatability: recurrence trigger = monthly close, refund/allowance events and tax handoff. Expected recurrence type = `same-customer` and `cross-customer`.
- Standardizable unit: channel intake map, exception taxonomy, evidence pack, QA checklist and handoff file.
- Leverage path: reusable templates/rules, standard intake, trained QA operators and selective automation. Founder-labor scaling risk = `medium`.
- Main contradiction: licensed accounting firms/ERP connectors may already solve most of the problem; operational exceptions may be too customer-specific.
- Biggest unknown: second-month repeat, fixed-scope acceptance and whether the work can stay outside regulated filing/representation.
- Cheapest behavioral test: offer a no-build, fixed-scope diagnostic/quote based on one redacted month and request an accepted quote or scheduled second-month continuation; no paid pilot is recommended in this run. Success signal: 2 comparable samples and 1 concrete commitment.
- Kill criterion: no buyer shares a sample or accepts a fixed scope; all work requires regulated accounting/tax services beyond founder scope; or each customer uses a different exception taxonomy.

- Startup classification: `research-more`.

## Not recommended now

- Generic AI document-to-data platform: service demand exists, but the document family and recurring schema are not proven.
- Generic “full-service” e-commerce agency: external transactions exist, but scope and labor are too broad for a cheap, defensible first test.
- Generic government tender proposal writer: completed external service exists, but the work is expertise-heavy and the proposed preflight wedge is unvalidated.
- General construction estimating platform: custom professional judgment and trust create high founder-labor scaling risk.

## Decision

The best two problems to carry into review are **fixed-scope e-commerce back-office operations** and **multi-channel e-commerce month-close/reconciliation**. The first has stronger comparable paid-transaction evidence but higher labor risk; the second has a clearer recurring workflow and leverage path but exact payment remains unproven. Both should be tested as narrow manual offers/commitment requests before any software work.
