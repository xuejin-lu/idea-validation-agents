## Candidate A — month-close / reconciliation

### What is established

- The workflow exists in Taiwan and consumes paid internal labor: 104 listings include channel settlement, bank/platform reconciliation, invoices, refunds/allowances, monthly close and accountant handoff.
- Externalization exists: the 2026-08 Tasker request asks for long-term external help after an internal finance role was removed.
- The monthly trigger is explicit in the buyer request and is not merely a generic inference.
- A safe manual boundary is plausible: collect/export data, normalize, tie out, flag exceptions and prepare a handoff packet. Formal bookkeeping, tax advice, filing, or representation must be excluded or performed by a qualified partner.
- Public research cannot establish that two unrelated businesses share the same schema or that the buyer will continue after the first month.

### Regulation/professional boundary

The Ministry of Finance rules state that registered bookkeeping/tax agents may be entrusted with commercial accounting, tax filings/applications and tax consultation, and require a mandate with scope and authority. The research therefore treats the unlicensed startup boundary as unresolved rather than assuming “reconciliation” is automatically safe. The first test should be limited to data preparation/QA/handoff and reviewed by a qualified accounting professional before delivery.

### Cheapest next behavioral test

- Exact offer: “one-month multi-channel reconciliation pack” for one Taiwan company using website + multiple channels; deliver a normalized input table, exception list, evidence links and accountant handoff checklist. Explicitly exclude tax filing, tax advice and formal bookkeeping representation.
- Exact target segment: companies matching the Tasker buyer pattern; do not assume a specific size or vertical.
- Acquisition channel: reply/list through Tasker or PRO360 intent categories, plus one Taiwanese e-commerce/accounting community or referral partner. Do not rely on cold calls/emails.
- Prospect must do: provide a redacted month of exports/invoices/refund data, confirm the channel list, and accept a written fixed-scope quote or schedule a paid continuation; no software build and no paid pilot is run in this phase.
- Strongest success signal: one accepted quote/commitment from a matching buyer plus a second buyer whose exception fields can be represented by the same checklist.
- Kill criterion: buyers require formal accounting/tax work outside the safe boundary; no buyer shares a redacted sample; every scope is bespoke; or no continuation is scheduled after the first pack.
- Result that justifies next phase: two matching buyers accept the same input/output contract, one commits to a second monthly cycle, and the work can be reviewed by a qualified accounting partner without founder-hours growing 1:1.

### Desk research saturation

`READY_FOR_REAL_WORLD_TEST`. The remaining decisive unknowns are behavioral-only: file access, accepted scope, commitment/payment and month-two repeat.

## Candidate B — fixed-scope back-office operations

### What is established

- Taiwan buyers actively request long-term platform operations, including listing, inventory/price updates, customer replies, order/shipping handling and campaign setup.
- PRO360 documents a broad e-commerce outsourcing market with completed-service reviews, contract and third-party payment workflow, and an “outsourced labor” mode that can include listing, packing/shipping, image editing and customer service. [PRO360](https://www.pro360.com.tw/category/ec_third_partner)
- A current Tasker request directly shows execution-only work: order processing, listing and customer replies for platform operations and shipping. This is a buyer request, not proof of payment.
- The broad category often bundles strategy, ads, creative, analytics and growth accountability; the proposed narrow package is therefore not yet category-proven.

### Labor and exception risk

The cleanest package candidate is one platform plus a fixed SKU/order band and explicit task SLA. Even then, platform changes, customer-specific copy, returns, inventory exceptions and urgent campaign work can expand scope. No public evidence currently proves that this package has enough margin or can be delegated without founder oversight. This is why the startup status is `DEMOTE_TO_SERVICE`.

### Why desk research should stop

Public listings can show that buyers ask for execution work, but cannot credibly resolve whether the same package produces acceptable margin, repeatability or low exception load. Searching for more broad agency pages would not answer those questions.

### If revisited later

Require one narrow task bundle to survive a real commitment test: one platform, one response SLA, one SKU/order band and a no-strategy/no-ads boundary. Promote only if two buyers accept the same scope and the operator-hours per unit stay bounded. Until then, treat B as a manual service discovery vehicle rather than a startup opportunity.

## Research saturation decision

- A: `READY_FOR_REAL_WORLD_TEST` — the next decisive evidence is behavioral.
- B: `DEMOTE_TO_SERVICE` — external demand is real, but startup leverage is not yet established; do not spend more desk-research cycles unless a narrower package is chosen.

No unrelated startup search was run because the finalists did not fail the hard Taiwan/evidence gates; they reached a meaningful comparison and saturation decision.
