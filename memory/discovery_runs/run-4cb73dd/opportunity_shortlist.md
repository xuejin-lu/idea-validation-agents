# Opportunity Shortlist

## Research constraints

- Taiwan-first; first prospects should be reachable through marketplaces, search, communities, referrals or partnerships rather than cold outbound.
- Default validation: 1–3 people, NT$0–5,000, up to 3 months, manual-first.
- This run does not recommend building software or running a paid pilot.
- Commercial stage is explicit: `interest`, `buyer_request`, `commitment`, `paid_transaction`.
- Category evidence is not silently rewritten as segment or exact-wedge evidence.
- Previous run preserved at `memory/discovery_runs/run-44ab644/`; older runs remain archived.

## Top Startup Opportunities

### Candidate A — 多通路電商的月結、發票與退款差異包

- Startup classification: `startup-ready-to-test` (medium confidence; exact package payment unproven).
- Target user: **Taiwan companies using a website plus multiple e-commerce channels**, exactly as represented by the recent buyer request; company size and vertical are not assumed.
- Economic buyer: brand owner, finance lead or operations lead.
- Problem: each month the team aggregates platform, payment, bank, ERP and invoice data, then handles refunds/allowances and prepares the accountant handoff. Existing bookkeeping may not own platform-level exceptions.
- Current workaround: internal e-commerce accountant, Excel/CSV reconciliation, platform exports, external bookkeeping/accounting handoff.
- Strongest evidence:
  - **Observed / buyer_request / Tier A, 2026-08-28:** Taiwan Tasker buyer requests long-term outsourcing after cancelling an internal finance role; scope includes multi-channel invoices, sales aggregation, reconciliation, refunds/allowances, monthly reports and accountant handoff. The displayed NT$6,000 budget and 15 proposals remain request-stage evidence. [Source](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=250%2C60%2C64)
  - **Observed / paid_labor / Tier A, 2026-06/07:** 104 listings assign platform/bank reconciliation, invoices, refunds/allowances, monthly close and accountant handoff; visible salaries include NT$30,000–33,000 and NT$45,000–60,000/month. [Source](https://www.104.com.tw/jobs/search/?jobcat=2003001000&keyword=%E5%B0%8D%E5%B8%B3%E5%96%AE)
  - **Observed / paid_transaction / Tier A, 2026-09:** PRO360 accounting category includes recent completed-service reviews and recurring outsourcing context; this is comparable accounting outsourcing, not proof of the exact e-commerce exception package. [Source](https://www.pro360.com.tw/category/accounting_service)
- Category demand: `observed`.
- Segment match: `observed` for the multi-channel company represented in the Tasker request; company size/vertical remain unknown. Segment evidence: [Tasker buyer request](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=250%2C60%2C64).
- Exact-wedge demand: `inferred`; the request includes the workflow but not a standardized productized package.
- External demand: observed; payment for the exact wedge: unproven. Comparable accounting payment: observed.
- Evidence quality: A/B mix with recent buyer request, independent paid labor and comparable paid transactions.
- Value mechanism: hours avoided, fewer monthly exceptions, faster handoff and lower error/leakage risk; no exact savings claimed.
- Recurrence: trigger = monthly close, refunds/allowances and accounting handoff. Expected recurrence = `same-customer` and `cross-customer`.
- Standardizable unit: channel intake map, exception taxonomy, evidence pack, QA checklist and handoff file.
- Leverage path: reusable rules/templates, standardized intake, trained QA operators and selective automation. Founder-labor scaling risk = `medium`.
- Main contradiction: licensed accounting firms and ERP connectors may already cover the need; customer-specific exceptions may defeat standardization.
- Biggest unknown: accepted quote/commitment for the exact package and whether the same customer repeats in month two.
- Cheapest behavioral test: show a fixed-scope quote based on one redacted month and seek an accepted quote or scheduled continuation; no build or paid pilot. Success signal: 2 comparable samples and 1 concrete commitment.
- Kill criterion: no buyer shares a sample or accepts a fixed scope; work crosses into unlicensed/regulated filing; or exception taxonomies are entirely different.

### Candidate B — 電商後台例行營運的固定工作包

- Startup classification: `startup-ready-to-test` (medium-low confidence; exact package and segment fit remain unproven).
- Target user: **Taiwan e-commerce businesses seeking long-term channel operations support**, as represented by current Tasker and PRO360 category evidence; not assumed to be microbrands.
- Economic buyer: company owner, e-commerce lead or operations manager.
- Problem: product listing, platform maintenance, orders, customer replies, inventory, returns and campaign setup recur; a broad full-service agency may be over-scoped for routine execution.
- Current workaround: internal e-commerce assistant, generalist employee, full-service operator, or manual platform back-office work.
- Strongest evidence:
  - **Observed / buyer_request / Tier A, 2026-08:** Tasker buyer requests long-term platform operations including listing, price/inventory management, page optimization, campaign activity and reporting; other recent listings request long-term product listing/customer reply support and monthly platform operations. [Source](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=133)
  - **Observed / buyer_request / Tier A, 2026-04/05:** other Tasker requests cover long-term e-commerce operations, customer complaints, payment/logistics coordination and inventory. [Source](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=133%2C140%2C447)
  - **Observed / paid_transaction / Tier A, 2026-09:** PRO360 e-commerce outsourcing category shows completed-service customer reviews and describes contract/third-party payment workflow; this proves category transactions, not the exact narrow work package. [Source](https://www.pro360.com.tw/category/ec_third_partner)
  - **Observed / paid_labor / Tier A, 2026-08/09:** 104 roles repeatedly pay for listings, platform maintenance, inventory, orders, returns, customer service and sales-data tracking. [Source](https://www.104.com.tw/jobs/search/?keyword=%E9%9B%BB%E5%95%86)
- Category demand: `observed`.
- Segment match: `observed` for Taiwan e-commerce businesses needing long-term operations; exact size/vertical is not established. Segment evidence: [Tasker category](https://www.tasker.com.tw/cases?selected_tags=133), [PRO360 category](https://www.pro360.com.tw/category/ec_third_partner).
- Exact-wedge demand: `inferred`; buyers request broad operations, not the proposed one-platform/one-band package.
- External demand: observed; payment for exact fixed package: unproven. Comparable category payment: observed.
- Evidence quality: A/B mix; multiple recent buyer requests plus completed category reviews and paid labor.
- Value mechanism: fewer missed updates, faster response/processing, fewer inventory/order errors and fewer owner interruptions; exact savings unknown.
- Recurrence: trigger = daily/weekly operations, campaign cycles, orders, returns and inventory exceptions. Expected recurrence = `same-customer` and `cross-customer`.
- Standardizable unit: one platform, fixed SKU/order band, response SLA, listing/update checklist and exception queue.
- Leverage path: SOPs, batch operations, templates, trained operators and selective automation. Founder-labor scaling risk = `medium-high`.
- Main contradiction: broad incumbents already bundle these tasks with strategy, creative, ads and performance accountability; narrow scope may be unattractive or still labor-linear.
- Biggest unknown: whether a buyer accepts a fixed recurring execution scope instead of a generalist or full-service agency.
- Cheapest behavioral test: publish one platform/one task bundle on an existing marketplace and seek an accepted recurring quote or scheduled start; no growth promise. Success signal: 3 qualified inquiries, 1 concrete commitment and repeatable task boundaries across 2 buyers.
- Kill criterion: every buyer asks for bespoke strategy/creative/ads; no fixed scope is accepted; or founder hours rise 1:1 with volume.

## Not recommended now

- Generic AI document-to-data platform: category service demand exists, but recurring document family and segment match are unproven.
- Generic full-service e-commerce agency: category transactions exist, but broad scope and labor risk weaken a low-cost startup test.
- Generic tender proposal writer: completed category transactions exist, but the proposed preflight wedge is unvalidated and expert-heavy.
- General construction estimating platform: custom judgment, trust and professional liability create high founder-labor risk.

## Decision

The two strongest problems to test are **multi-channel e-commerce month-close/reconciliation** and **fixed-scope e-commerce back-office operations**. The first has clearer recurring workflow and leverage but only buyer-request evidence for the exact wedge. The second has more recent recurring buyer requests and category paid transactions but higher labor risk. Both require segment and exact-wedge commitment evidence before any build decision.
