# Research Gates — Permanent Rules

These gates are persistent across research phases. They are not a one-run task list.

## 1. Taiwan Gate
Primary market is Taiwan. Foreign evidence may inspire hypotheses but cannot prove Taiwan demand, payment, regulation, or buyer behavior.

## 2. Evidence Roles
Every material evidence item must have one primary role:
- `pain`: target user directly experiences the problem/workaround.
- `paid_labor`: people are paid internally to perform the workflow.
- `buyer_request`: buyer actively seeks an outside provider/quote/service; not proof of payment.
- `transaction`: completed purchase/paid engagement/verified completed service.
- `competition`: alternative/vendor exists.
- `regulatory_context`: legal/process requirement exists.
- `market_context`: infrastructure/size/attention context.

Government rules and vendor pricing do not by themselves prove pain or demand.

## 3. Commercial Proof Ladder
Use the strongest verified stage only:
1. `interest`
2. `buyer_request`
3. `commitment`
4. `paid_transaction`

A posted budget or proposal count remains `buyer_request`. Never infer payment.

Every candidate must state separately:
- external demand: observed | unproven
- payment/commitment: observed | unproven

## 4. Problem Evidence Gate
To advance beyond research-more, require at least two independent Taiwan-specific sources supporting pain, paid labor, buyer request, or transaction. At least one must be demand-side or paid-labor evidence.

## 5. Externalizability Gate
Internal paid work is not automatically a market for a startup.

Top candidates need recent Taiwan-specific evidence that buyers seek, buy, outsource, or concretely request outside help. A job posting alone cannot satisfy this gate.

## 6. Recency Gate
Prefer buyer-side evidence from the last 24 months. Older evidence may support workflow continuity but cannot be the sole direct pain/external-demand signal.

## 7. Segment-Match Gate
Keep separate:
- category demand
- proposed target segment
- exact wedge

For every candidate output:
- `category_demand: observed | unproven`
- `target_segment`
- `segment_match: observed | inferred | unproven`
- `segment_evidence`
- `exact_wedge_demand: observed | inferred | unproven`

Never shrink broad category evidence into a narrow ICP without direct support.

## 8. Repeatability & Leverage Gate
A paid service is not automatically a startup.

A Top Startup Opportunity needs credible recurrence or cross-customer standardization plus a leverage path through templates, delegation, automation, standardized intake/output, or software-assisted processing.

Explicitly record:
- recurrence trigger
- recurrence type: same-customer | cross-customer | none/unknown
- standardizable unit
- leverage path
- founder-labor scaling risk: low | medium | high

Classify each candidate as:
- `startup-ready-to-test`
- `service-ready-to-test`
- `research-more`
- `insufficient`

## 9. Founder Constraints Gate
Always obey `FOUNDER_CONSTRAINTS.md`, especially:
- Taiwan only
- validation budget ≤ NT$5,000
- validation horizon ≤ 3 months
- online-accessible prospects
- avoid business models dependent primarily on cold call/cold email
- manual-first is allowed
- niche markets are acceptable
- technology/AI is a means, not the objective

## 10. Research Saturation Gate
Desk research must stop when the remaining critical unknown cannot be resolved credibly from public evidence.

Classify each critical unknown:
- `desk_researchable`
- `behavioral_only`

Examples of behavioral-only unknowns:
- will a buyer accept this exact scope?
- will a buyer share real files?
- will a buyer commit or pay?
- will the same buyer return next month?
- will a standardized package survive real exceptions?

Do not keep searching the web to simulate behavioral proof.

When the strongest remaining unknowns are behavioral-only, output:
`READY_FOR_REAL_WORLD_TEST`
and design the smallest falsifiable test instead of another broad discovery run.

## 11. No Premature Build
Do not recommend software implementation before exact-wedge demand, repeated scope, and a plausible leverage path have survived a behavioral test.
